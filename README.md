# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project** focused on analyzing customer shopping behavior and converting raw customer data into meaningful business insights using **Python, Pandas, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to understand purchasing patterns, customer segments, product performance, subscription behavior, discounts, repeat purchases, and customer satisfaction.

The dataset contains **3,900 customer records and 18 columns**.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow, starting with data cleaning and feature engineering in Python, followed by PostgreSQL database integration, SQL-based business analysis, and interactive visualization in Power BI.

### Project Workflow

**Raw Data → Python & Pandas → Data Cleaning → Feature Engineering → PostgreSQL → SQL Analysis → Power BI → Business Insights**

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Clean and prepare raw customer shopping data.
* Identify and handle missing values.
* Standardize the dataset for analysis.
* Create additional features for customer behavior analysis.
* Store the cleaned data in PostgreSQL.
* Perform business-oriented analysis using SQL.
* Analyze customer purchasing and subscription behavior.
* Identify high-performing products and customer segments.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

# 🐍 Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering

Python and Pandas were used to prepare the raw dataset for further analysis.

### Key Steps

* Loaded and explored the dataset.
* Examined the dataset structure and data types.
* Assessed missing values.
* Identified **37 missing values in the Review Rating column**.
* Applied category-level median imputation to handle missing ratings.
* Standardized column names using `snake_case`.
* Removed redundant information such as the `Promo Code Used` column.
* Created an **Age** feature.
* Created a **Frequency Mapping** feature.
* Prepared the cleaned dataset for PostgreSQL integration.

---

# 🗄️ PostgreSQL Integration

After data cleaning and feature engineering, the prepared dataset was connected to PostgreSQL.

The cleaned data was loaded into the **`customer` table** and used as the foundation for SQL-based business analysis.

PostgreSQL provided a structured database environment for querying customer transactions and generating analytical results.

---

# 💻 SQL Business Analysis

The project includes **10 business questions** analyzed using SQL.

The questions were handled in the following order:

### Q1. Revenue by Gender

What is the total revenue generated by male vs. female customers?

### Q2. Discounted Purchases Above Average

Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?

### Q3. Top-Rated Products

Which are the top 5 products with the highest average review rating?

### Q4. Shipping Comparison

Compare the average Purchase Amounts between Standard and Express Shipping.

### Q5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers

Do subscribed customers spend more? Compare average spend and total revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.

### Q6. Discount Rate by Product

Which 5 products have the highest percentage of purchases with discounts applied?

### Q7. Customer Segmentation

Segment customers into New, Returning, and Loyal based on their total number of previous purchases, and show the count of each segment.

### Q8. Top Products by Category

What are the top 3 most purchased products within each category?

### Q9. Repeat Buyers & Subscription

Are customers who are repeat buyers, with more than 5 previous purchases, also likely to subscribe?

### Q10. Revenue by Age Group

What is the revenue contribution of each age group?

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 💰 Revenue Performance

Approximately **4K customers** generated **$233.08K** in total revenue, with an average purchase value of **$59.76**.

### 🔁 Repeat Customers

Approximately **3K customers** are repeat buyers, highlighting the importance of customer retention and loyalty strategies.

### 👥 Subscription Behavior

Only **27%** of customers are subscribed, compared with **73%** non-subscribers. This indicates an opportunity to improve subscription adoption.

### 🎯 Age Group Performance

The **Young-Adult** segment is the top-performing age group based on revenue contribution.

### ⭐ Product Performance

**Boots** is the highest-rated product based on customer review ratings.

### 😊 Customer Satisfaction

The overall average customer rating is **3.75**, providing a useful indicator of customer satisfaction and product experience.

# 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions can be considered:

* Increase subscription adoption through stronger benefits, targeted offers, and personalized campaigns.
* Strengthen customer retention strategies for repeat buyers through loyalty programs.
* Focus targeted marketing campaigns on the **Young-Adult** segment.
* Promote highly rated products such as **Boots**.
* Monitor product-level ratings to identify opportunities for improving customer satisfaction.
* Increase the average purchase value through cross-selling, bundling, and personalized recommendations.
* Continue using interactive dashboards to monitor customer behavior and business performance.
